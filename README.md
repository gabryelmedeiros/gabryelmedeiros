🔬 **Research (PROSAICO lab, UERJ)**
 
* **MPGBP quantization:** post-training quantization of weights into sums of powers of two, with no retraining. On VGG-16 (ImageNet): 72.03% accuracy (baseline 72.18%) with 53.3% of weights zeroed and an estimated 2.63 bits/parameter, about 12x below FP32. Published at SBrT 2026 ([paper](https://biblioteca.sbrt.org.br/articles/5229)).
* **Federated learning simulator** with gradient compression: ~75% less network traffic while keeping 90.5% accuracy. SBrT 2025.
